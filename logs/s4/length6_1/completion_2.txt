Operator plan: Parse DT_STRATA to datetime and format as MM/DD/YYYY for the 'Date' column. Rename the 24 hourly percentage columns (PCT_HOURLY_0100 to PCT_HOURLY_2400) to 'Hr 1' through 'Hr 24'. Discard other columns.

```python
import pandas as pd
def transform(source: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    out = pd.DataFrame()
    dates = pd.to_datetime(source['DT_STRATA'], format='%m/%d/%Y')
    out['Date'] = dates.dt.strftime('%m/%d/%Y')
    
    for h in range(1, 25):
        src_col = f'PCT_HOURLY_{h:02d}00'
        tgt_col = f'Hr {h}'
        out[tgt_col] = source[src_col]
        
    return out
```

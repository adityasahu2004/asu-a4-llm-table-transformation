Operator plan: Parse CST to extract date; format as MM/DD/YYYY for Date column; rename the 24 hourly columns to Hr 1 through Hr 24.

```python
import pandas as pd
def transform(source: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    out = pd.DataFrame(index=source.index)
    dates = pd.to_datetime(source['CST'], format='%a %m/%d/%Y')
    out['Date'] = dates.dt.strftime('%m/%d/%Y')
    for h in range(1, 25):
        src_col = f'{h}:00' if h < 24 else '24:00:00'
        out[f'Hr {h}'] = source[src_col]
    return out.reset_index(drop=True)
```
